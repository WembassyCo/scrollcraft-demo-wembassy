Our Story

Built by operators who understand the weight of stewardship.

Wembassy was founded on a simple observation: family offices were managing eight-figure portfolios with tools built for small businesses. Spreadsheets. Shared drives. Disconnected SaaS apps.

We build the infrastructure that replaces all of it — secure, purpose-built platforms that give families and their advisors a single source of truth. Every contact, every decision, every opportunity, in one place.

We don't ship templates. We engineer systems.
